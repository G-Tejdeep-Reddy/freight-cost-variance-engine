An end-to-end supply chain analytics pipeline and dual-frontend control tower designed to track freight spend, detect contract variances, audit carrier service level agreements (SLAs), and eliminate cost leakage across multi-modal transport networks.

---

## 🏢 Business Problem
Shipping operations frequently suffer from invisible financial drainage caused by:
- **Cost Leakage:** Actual rates paid to carriers frequently surpassing pre-negotiated baseline contracts due to unmonitored spot-market exposure.
- **Multi-Modal Blind Spots:** Managing Ocean, Air, and Road transport under a single lens without accounting for structural cost and speed disparities.
- **Vendor Accountability Gaps:** Lack of centralized visibility to penalize underperforming carriers during annual contract (RFP) negotiations.

---

## 🏗️ How It Was Built (Project Flow)

1. **Star-Schema Modeling (PostgreSQL):** Designed a robust relational database structure (`dim_shipping_lanes` and `fact_freight_transactions`) to prevent data anomalies and manage multi-modal shipping logs efficiently.
2. **Data Pipeline & Metrics (Python & SQL):** Cleaned transactional logs and programmatically computed dynamic financial metrics—specifically **Cost Leakage** (`variance_vs_contract_usd`) by subtracting contracted base rates from actual spot rates.
3. **Custom Web Application (Streamlit):** Developed and deployed a Python-driven web app (`app.py`) allowing users to interactively query multi-modal shipping performance, view corridor breakdowns, and filter by carrier tiers.
4. **Executive Control Tower (Power BI Service):** Developed and published an analytics dashboard featuring executive KPI summary cards, route-level Trade Lane audit matrices, carrier performance rankings, and monthly cost trend lines.

---

## 📊 Key Features & Visualizations
- **Executive KPI Cards:** Real-time visibility into Total Shipments, Total Freight Spend, Average Transit Days, and Total Cost Leakage.
- **Trade Lane Corridor Matrix:** Detailed route-level auditing (`origin_hub` to `destination_hub`) comparing shipment volume against actual spend and variance.
- **Carrier Performance Ranking:** Horizontal bar charts isolating vendors driving the highest cost overruns and tracking SLA compliance.
- **Multi-Modal Slicers:** Instant dynamic filtering across Ocean, Air, and Road freight.

## 💡 Key Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations

Based on the variance analysis and cost leakage audit report generated by the engine across 1,260 shipments, the total contract cost variance sits at **\$278,224.79**, with **56.4%** of all transactions flagged for high overspend. The key cost leakages and mitigation strategies are broken down below:

### 1. Primary Areas of Cost Leakage (Where the Company is Losing Money)
* **Air Freight Market Volatility:** Air transport accounts for the largest share of financial leakage, driving **\$156,593.80 (56.3%)** of total contract variance across the network, despite handling smaller total physical volumes than ocean or road.
* **High-Concentration Lane Overspends:** Two primary air corridors account for over half of all network cost leakage:
  * **LANE-004 (Mumbai BOM → Frankfurt FRA):** Generated **\$90,963.78** in overspends (**32.7%** of total network variance).
  * **LANE-005 (Delhi DEL → Dubai DXB):** Contributed **\$65,630.02** (**23.6%** of total network variance).
* **Ocean Route Bleed:** On ocean lanes, **LANE-002 (Cochin Port → Hamburg Port)** is the largest single leak, accumulating **\$46,749.86** in excess costs (**16.8%** of total variance). Overall, ocean shipping accounts for **\$95,820.01 (34.4%)** of network variance.
* **High Overspend Frequency:** **711 out of 1,260 shipments (56.4%)** breached standard variance tolerances, heavily driven by carriers charging premium rates above baseline contracts during spot market spikes.

### 2. Actionable Cost-Reduction Strategy (Where to Cut Expenses)
* **Re-index Core Air Freight Contracts:** Immediately re-negotiate baseline contract terms on high-variance air corridors (`LANE-004` and `LANE-005`), targeting key carriers showing high mean variances per shipment (*Emirates Skycargo*, *DHL Aviation*, *FedEx Express*).
* **Enforce Automated Anomaly Flags:** Implement automated hard-stop flags in `detect_anomalies.py` for any transaction where actual rates deviate past defined tolerance bands before invoice settlement.
* **Optimize Ocean Carrier Allocation:** Reallocate volume on secondary ocean lanes toward lower-variance contracted partners (e.g., *Maersk* or *CMA CGM*) to control spot exposure on high-cost routes like Cochin to Hamburg.
